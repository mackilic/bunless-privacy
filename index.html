Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Bunless ("the app") is a keto restaurant-assistance app. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • Profile info (name): Entered during onboarding and stored only on your device (Android SharedPreferences). It is never sent to us or any third party. There is no Bunless server and no account system — your profile exists only to personalize your on-device experience.
  • Macro targets & meals: Stored only on your device.
  • Location: Used only on-device to find nearby restaurants via the Geoapify Places API. Your coordinates are sent to Geoapify's API as part of that request (subject to Geoapify's Privacy Policy); they are not sent to us.

Third parties

  • Geoapify Places API: When you grant location permission, the app queries Geoapify to list nearby restaurants. No Bunless-owned servers are involved. Location data shown is © OpenMapTiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers.

Data retention & deletion

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes everything. Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings also deletes everything.

Children

Bunless is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and ship with the app.
